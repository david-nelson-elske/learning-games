# Tank quiz

[tank-quiz.mp4](https://github.com/david-nelson-elske/learning-games/releases/download/media-v1/tank-quiz.mp4), 55 seconds.

Tanks fire at targets, then three easy questions, each with a three-dot countdown to think before the answer:

1. Two tanks and one more: 2 + 1 = 3.
2. Four tanks, one goes boom: 4 − 1 = 3, a first look at taking away.
3. Three tanks here and three there: 3 + 3 = 6.

**Tip:** pause during the countdown and let the child answer out loud.

The narration and pictures are in [script.md](script.md).
